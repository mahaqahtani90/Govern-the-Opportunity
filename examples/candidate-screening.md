# Worked example — Candidate screening

## Task

Produce an initial structured comparison of candidate applications against published job requirements. The system does not reject candidates or make the hiring decision.

## Opportunity assessment

| Dimension | Illustrative score | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| AI Fit | 4.3 | Language-intensive, repeatable, and reviewable output |
| Business Value | 4.0 | Potential cycle-time and consistency improvement |
| Readiness | 3.0 | Depends on job criteria, data quality, and integration |
| **Opportunity** | **3.8 — Medium** | Promising but readiness must improve |

## Initial risk screen

| Domain | Illustrative score | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Data sensitivity | 4 | Applications contain personal data |
| Impact on individuals or rights | 5 | Employment opportunity may be affected |
| Consequence of error | 5 | A qualified person could be disadvantaged |
| Legal or regulatory significance | 4 | Employment and data obligations may apply |
| Security and misuse | 3 | Unauthorized access or prompt misuse is plausible |
| Explainability and contestability | 5 | Criteria and human review must be available |
| **Initial risk** | **5 — High** | Highest material risk controls routing |

## AI TaskFit route

**Advise only — detailed assessment required.**

Minimum governance conditions before any pilot:

- No autonomous rejection or ranking with operational effect
- Documented job-related criteria
- Human decision owner and mandatory review
- Approved personal-data handling
- Bias and subgroup performance evaluation
- Missing-data behavior and appeal/escalation process
- Separate test evidence and predefined acceptance thresholds

This example illustrates the central AI TaskFit rule: high potential does not cancel high impact.
