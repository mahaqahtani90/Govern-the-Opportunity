# Govern the Opportunity — Project Governance

## Status

The AI TaskFit 5D Method v0.1 is a public draft under the Govern the Opportunity principle and AI Opportunity Governance Framework — Layer 1 of AI Governance. Its scoring boundaries and decision matrix are subject to validation.

## Roles

- **Lead maintainer:** protects purpose, safety principles, release integrity, and attribution.
- **Maintainers:** review issues and proposed changes.
- **Contributors:** submit evidence, translations, examples, and tools.
- **Reviewers:** provide domain, legal, risk, human-factors, security, or technical review.

## Decision principles

Changes are evaluated for:

1. Simplicity and cross-sector usefulness
2. Evidence quality
3. Safety and rights impact
4. Auditability
5. Compatibility with the core distinction between opportunity and risk

No scoring change may reduce a high-impact case to a less restrictive route solely because business value is high.

## Releases

- `0.x` releases are public drafts and may change.
- `1.0` requires documented field validation, expert review, resolved core definitions, and stable tool compatibility.
- Material changes are recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Conflicts of interest

Reviewers and maintainers should disclose material interests in products, vendors, or assessments affected by a proposal.
